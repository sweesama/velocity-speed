隐私政策与离线数据承诺

Privacy Policy & On-Device Commitment

EFFECTIVE: 2026-11-24 URL: https://velocitygps.dev CONTACT: sweeyeah@gmail.com
01

核心原则：100% 本地计算，零服务器上传

Core Principle: 100% On-Device Processing, Zero Server Uploads

Velocity（由独立开发者运营，官方网站 https://velocitygps.dev）是一款纯本地实时 GPS 测速、几何海拔与前挡玻璃 HUD 仪器。我们不设任何账户系统、不要求手机号或邮箱登录，也不会向任何远程服务器上传、存储或出售您的实时位置、移动轨迹、海拔高度或照片内容。

Velocity (operated at https://velocitygps.dev) is a strictly on-device GPS speedometer, geometric altimeter, and windshield HUD instrument. We do not operate user accounts, do not require sign-in, and never upload, store, sell, or share your live location, travel routes, altitude, or imported photos to any remote server.

02

位置与卫星定位数据（GPS / Geolocation）

Location & Satellite Telemetry (GPS / CoreLocation / Geolocation)

仅当您在使用期间明确授予位置权限（“使用 App 期间允许”或浏览器定位权限）后，Velocity 才会读取设备芯片输出的多普勒速度、WGS84 空间几何海拔、航向角与经纬度。

Only when you grant location permission while using the app will Velocity read your device's hardware Doppler speed, WGS84 geometric altitude, heading, and coordinates.

  • 实时本地解算：所有速度平滑滤波、累计里程与最高速度记录仅在您的设备内存中实时计算，关闭会话后不留存任何云端轨迹。
  • 支持飞行模式：卫星接收为被动式本地硬件信号读取，断网及开启飞行模式时仍完全在本地工作。
  • Local memory only: Kalman smoothing, trip distance, and peak speed are calculated solely in your device's local memory and never transmitted externally.
  • Offline by design: Satellite reception is passive and works offline or in Airplane Mode without transmitting data.
03

运动与加速度传感器（IMU / DeviceMotion）

Motion & Accelerometer Sensors (CoreMotion / DeviceMotion)

当您在仪表盘或设置页主动激活“推背感传感器”时，系统仅在本地读取设备线性加速度传感器以实时估算纵向 G 值及加速计时。该传感器数据不会持久化写入云端或共享给第三方。

When you explicitly activate the G-Force sensor, Velocity reads local device motion/accelerometer data solely to render live forward acceleration (G-force) on screen. Motion sensor readings are never persisted externally or shared with third parties.

04

相册与实景照片水印（Photos / File Input）

Photos & Watermark Studio

当您在“速度证书”页主动选择一张本地照片作为背景合拍测速海报时，该图片仅通过浏览器/设备本地 HTML5 Canvas 在内存中进行图文合成与导出保存。您的照片绝不会离开您的手机或上传到任何服务器。

When you choose a photo from your library inside Speed Card Studio, the image is composited locally on your device using HTML5 Canvas strictly to export your poster image. Your photos never leave your device.

05

本地存储与您的控制权（Local Storage）

On-Device Storage & Data Deletion Control

您的界面语言、速度单位（km/h、mph、kt、m/s）、海拔单位、HUD 偏好、当前测速记录摘要及离线 Pro 激活状态，均仅保存在您设备本地存储（localStorage）与 Service Worker 离线缓存中。您可以随时在应用内点击「清零当前测速记录」、撤销系统定位权限，或通过清除网站数据 / 卸载应用彻底删除所有本地数据。

Your language choice, unit preferences, HUD mirror settings, session summaries, and offline Pro pass state are stored strictly on-device via local storage and Service Worker offline caches. You can delete all local data anytime by tapping "Reset Current Session", revoking permissions, or clearing site/app data.

06

第三方服务与零广告追踪

Third-Party Services & Zero Ad Tracking

Velocity 未集成任何广告 SDK、跨应用追踪标识符（IDFA / AAID）或行为画像 SDK。网页版通过 Cloudflare 全球边缘网络分发静态资源，可能采用无 Cookie 的匿名聚合访问统计（如页面总体浏览量与地区分布）以评估站点可用性，不包含任何可识别个人身份的信息（PII）。

Velocity integrates zero ad networks, zero cross-app tracking identifiers (IDFA/AAID), and zero personal profiling SDKs. Static assets are served via Cloudflare Pages edge infrastructure, which may record privacy-preserving, cookie-free aggregate traffic metrics without personally identifiable information.

07

儿童隐私与联系我们

Children's Privacy & Contact

Velocity 为面向大众用户的通用仪表工具，不主动面向 13 周岁以下儿童收集任何个人信息。如您对本隐私政策或权限使用有任何疑问，欢迎随时通过电子邮件联系开发者：sweeyeah@gmail.com。

Velocity is a general-audience utility instrument and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you have any privacy questions, please contact the developer at sweeyeah@gmail.com.
