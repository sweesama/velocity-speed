QUICK START · 四大核心场景
QUICK START · KEY MODES
01 · AVIATION

万米高空飞行模式直测

Airplane Mode Cabin Reception

客机金属机身会屏蔽卫星波段。请在飞行模式下将手机贴近舷窗玻璃停留 10–30 秒，即可稳定锁定巡航速度与真实几何海拔。

Commercial fuselages shield GPS signals. Hold your phone directly against the cabin window glass for 10–30 seconds in Airplane Mode to lock ground speed and true altitude.

02 · RAIL & ROAD

高铁、驾车与静止防漂移

High-Speed Rail & Road Trips

系统内置卡尔曼滤波与静止门限，等待静止时不空转计时；检测到有效移动后自动启动行程统计，支持 km/h、mph、kt 与 m/s 一键切换。

Kalman smoothing eliminates stationary drift and pauses duration automatically when stopped. Tap the speed unit anytime to cycle km/h, mph, kt, and m/s.

03 · OPTICAL HUD

夜间前挡风玻璃投影

Windshield Optical HUD

点击主屏右上角 HUD 进入纯黑镜像投影模式，提供光学白、钛金属与香槟金三档色温。平放于仪表台，挡风玻璃即可反射出正向数字。

Tap HUD in the top right for high-contrast mirrored projection in White, Titanium, or Champagne tones. Lay the phone flat under the windshield at night.

04 · OFFLINE PWA

添加到桌面 · 断网秒开

Install as Offline Web App

iPhone 用户可在 Safari 点击底部「分享」›「添加到主屏幕」；Android Chrome 可点击右上角菜单「安装应用」，从此支持全屏无边框与断网打开。

On iOS Safari tap Share › Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome tap Menu › Install App for a full-screen, offline-ready experience.

FAQ · 常见问题排查
为什么提示“未开启定位”或速度一直显示 0？ Why does it show "No Access" or stay at 0?

1. iPhone / iPad：前往「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 定位服务」，确认总开关开启，并允许 Safari 浏览器（或 Velocity App）“使用 App 期间访问位置”并开启“精确位置”。
2. 安卓 / 微信浏览器：确保系统定位开关已开启，并在浏览器地址栏左侧图标处允许访问位置；在室内水泥墙环境下请靠近窗户或移至室外开阔处。

1. iOS: Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services and set access to "While Using" with Precise Location enabled.
2. Android / Desktop: Enable device GPS and click the site icon left of the URL bar to allow Location. Indoors, move near a window or outdoors.

手机微信或 Safari 点击保存海报后找不到图片怎么办？ How do I save Speed Card posters on iPhone or WeChat?

点击证书页右上角「↓ 保存」后，系统会直接弹出高清海报大图。手机用户请直接长按画面上的海报图片，选择「存储到“照片”」或「保存图片」，也可直接点击下方「调用系统分享」。

After tapping Save, a high-resolution image sheet opens automatically. On mobile, long-press the poster to save directly to Photos, or tap Share Image.

如何获得或兑换「FOUNDER 创始内测码」？ How do I redeem a Founding Beta Invite Code?

Visit the Beta Registration page to submit your email for Google Play Closed Testing or TestFlight access and receive your personal VELO-XXXX-XXXX pass key.
